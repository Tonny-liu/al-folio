## Meteorological and climate modeling datasets

Interested in exploring meteorological and climate patterns by yourself? Here are some global and regional datasets to get you started.

<div class="resource-list">
  <div class="resource-item">
    <div class="resource-item-header">
      <h3><a href="https://www.ecmwf.int/en/forecasts/dataset/ecmwf-reanalysis-v5" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">ERA5</a></h3>
      <span class="resource-badge">Reanalysis</span>
    </div>
    <p>ERA5 provides hourly and monthly estimates for a wide range of atmospheric, land, and oceanic climate variables, with global coverage at 31 km resolution from 1940 to present.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="resource-item">
    <div class="resource-item-header">
      <h3><a href="https://wcrp-cmip.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">CMIP</a></h3>
      <span class="resource-badge">Model Intercomparison</span>
    </div>
    <p>The Coupled Model Intercomparison Project brings together climate models from centres worldwide to compare results and improve understanding of past and future climate changes from natural and human influences.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="resource-item">
    <div class="resource-item-header">
      <h3><a href="https://cordex.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">CORDEX</a></h3>
      <span class="resource-badge">Regional Downscaling</span>
    </div>
    <p>CORDEX coordinates regional climate downscaling from reanalyses and CMIPs, covering Africa, Asia, Australia, the Americas, Europe, and nearby oceans.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="resource-item">
    <div class="resource-item-header">
      <h3><a href="https://www.worldclim.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">WorldClim</a></h3>
      <span class="resource-badge">Observational</span>
    </div>
    <p>WorldClim is a high-resolution global weather and climate dataset that provides monthly temperature and precipitation data for different periods.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="resource-item">
    <div class="resource-item-header">
      <h3><a href="https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Copernicus CDS</a></h3>
      <span class="resource-badge">Data Service</span>
    </div>
    <p>The Climate Data Store provides access to a wide range of quality-assured climate datasets, tools, and documentation from the Copernicus Climate Change Service.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Software & tools

Some useful tools and libraries to assist with your research and coding tasks.

<div class="resource-list">
  <div class="resource-item">
    <div class="resource-item-header">
      <h3><a href="https://github.com/hanschen/vim-ipython-cell" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">vim-ipython-cell</a></h3>
      <span class="resource-badge badge-software"><i class="fab fa-github"></i> Open Source</span>
    </div>
    <p>A Vim/Neovim plugin that allows you to run Python code in the IPython interactive terminal shell — great for scientific computing workflows.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="resource-item">
    <div class="resource-item-header">
      <h3><a href="https://unidata.github.io/netcdf4-python/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">netCDF4-python</a></h3>
      <span class="resource-badge badge-software"><i class="fab fa-python"></i> Python</span>
    </div>
    <p>A Python interface to the netCDF C library. Essential for reading and writing climate and Earth science data in the widely used NetCDF format.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="resource-item">
    <div class="resource-item-header">
      <h3><a href="https://docs.xarray.dev/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">xarray</a></h3>
      <span class="resource-badge badge-software"><i class="fab fa-python"></i> Python</span>
    </div>
    <p>Makes working with labelled multi-dimensional arrays in Python simple, efficient, and fun. A core tool for climate data analysis.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="resource-item">
    <div class="resource-item-header">
      <h3><a href="https://scitools-iris.readthedocs.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Iris</a></h3>
      <span class="resource-badge badge-software"><i class="fab fa-python"></i> Python</span>
    </div>
    <p>A powerful, format-agnostic Python library for analysing and visualising meteorological and oceanographic data sets.</p>
  </div>
</div>
